SS1 – SS3 Weekly Timetable — Overlap View

All three classes shown side-by-side per period, so any teacher clash is visible directly. 40-minute periods · Mon & Tue extended to 4:00pm · Break 11:50–12:30
Joint (whole class) Science dept Commercial dept Arts dept
No teacher clashes. Every teacher is booked into at most one class per period across the full week.
Rules applied this round:
  • Arts/Commercial-needs-Science (Mon/Tue, SS1): no Arts or Commercial dept-slot runs without a Science subject alongside it. Chemistry fills this role wherever T1 is free; where it couldn't reach, Biology or Government/History cover it instead.
  • SS1's DT is now split: 2 periods/week stay with T1, 2 periods/week move to a new teacher, T12, dedicated to DT.
  • Global max-2-consecutive-periods rule now enforced for every teacher/subject, with T1 alone permitted up to 3 in a row (and only on his own teaching days).
  • Biology may never be the only department running in a slot (always paired with Arts or Commercial) and never runs 3+ in a row.
  • Math and English are placed as double (back-to-back) periods wherever their weekly count allows, with any remainder as single periods.
Two open shortfalls:
  • SS1 Chemistry runs at 5/week against the 8/week boost target. Moving 2 of SS1's DT periods to the new teacher T12 freed some of T1's capacity, but not enough to fully close the gap.
  • English runs at 4/week for SS2 and SS3 instead of the target 5/week — T10's no-Friday rule plus the new max-2-consecutive cap left no legal slot for the 5th period once doubling was applied. SS1's English (4/week) is unaffected and met in full.
