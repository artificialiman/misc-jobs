SS1–SS3 Weekly Timetable — Two T1 Availability Variants

T1 fully unavailable Wednesday and Thursday (no periods at all those two days, including 2:30–4pm). Mon/Tue/Thu extended to 4:00pm for periods 9–10 (whitelisted subjects only; on Thursday this window is for DT/Livestock/Math/English relief — T1 himself does not use it in Variant A).
Joint (whole class) Science dept Commercial dept Arts dept
Zero teacher clashes. Arts/Commercial never runs without Science present, anywhere, all week. No back-to-back cap broken (T1's own subjects may run 3-in-a-row, Accounts exempt entirely). Biology never stands alone. Periods 9–10 hold only whitelisted subjects. Further-Maths/Agric guaranteed on Friday for every class (3/week total).
New this round: Literature-in-English (T13) added to the Arts department, forked with History in the same dept-slot (student takes one or the other) — provisional 2/week each, flagged for revision once T13's own schedule constraints are known. T13's availability is currently modeled as fully open.
Honest shortfalls:
  • Chemistry_ext/SS2: short by 2 period(s)/week
  • Chemistry_ext/SS3: short by 2 period(s)/week
  • Government/SS3: short by 1 period(s)/week
  • History/SS2: short by 1 period(s)/week
  • LitInEng/SS2: short by 1 period(s)/week
  • CRS/SS3: short by 1 period(s)/week
Chemistry_ext (T1 covering SS2/SS3 Chemistry at 2:30–4:00) is accepted at 0/week — T1 is already fully booked (22/22 slots) across his available days before this extra chunk gets a turn. This was flagged as aspirational, not a hard requirement, and is left unforced rather than displacing something else of his. The remaining shortfalls (History, Lit-in-Eng, CRS, Government, Yoruba — 1–2 periods/week each) are the ongoing cost of requiring Science alongside every Arts/Commercial dept-slot.
